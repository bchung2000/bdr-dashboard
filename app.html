Call Scripts

Openers, gatekeeper paths, voicemails, booking language, and discovery — versioned and ready.

Cold open · pattern interrupt
"Hey, it's [name] — you weren't expecting my call, so I'll be quick. I work with distribution ops leaders on [specific pain from intel]. Did I catch you at a terrible time, or just a bad one?"
Gatekeeper path
"I promised [exec]'s team a benchmark before Friday — could you point me to whoever owns her calendar?"
Voicemail · 18 seconds
"[Name], one idea about the new facility and a number worth hearing: 9,000 hours. I'll try you Thursday at 10 — or grab me sooner at [number]."
Booking the meeting
"Sounds like there's something here. I'm not asking you to evaluate anything — 20 minutes with our solutions consultant to pressure-test the math. Tuesday or Thursday better?"
Discovery questions
What does a mis-ship cost you today? · Who feels peak season first? · If the new facility slips a quarter, what breaks? · What did the last systems project teach the team?

Playbook

Objection Handling

The counters that work, organized by objection — not by wishful thinking.

"We just did a big systems project"
Validate, then reframe: "That's exactly why this works — it rides on top of what you just built. The question is what the DC team gave up in the migration."
"No budget this year"
"Totally fair. What's the manual workaround costing in the meantime? If the math says the do-nothing option is the expensive one, the budget conversation changes."
"Send me some information"
"Happy to — but generic decks waste your time. Give me 90 seconds on your pick process and I'll send the one page that's actually relevant."
"We're happy with our current system"
"Most of our best customers were too — until they saw what 'happy' was costing per order. Worth a benchmark just to know your number?"
