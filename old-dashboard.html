Call Scripts & Questions
Talk tracks, discovery questions, and booking scripts
Opening Line
"Hey [Name], this is Brian calling from Infor — did I catch you at a bad time?"
Quick Pitch (After They Say Go Ahead)
"The reason I'm reaching out is I work with distribution companies to help them modernize their warehouse operations with a Tier 1 WMS. We've been helping companies reduce labor costs and increase throughput with AI-driven automation — and I wanted to see if that's something on the radar for [Company] right now."
Bridge to Discovery
"I'm not looking to pitch you today — I just wanted to ask a couple quick questions to see if it's even worth a conversation. Is warehouse technology something your team is evaluating this year?"
If They Say Yes
"That's great to hear. I'd love to set up 30 minutes with you and one of our WMS specialists to learn more about what you're looking at and show you what we've been doing for companies like yours. Does [Day] or [Day] work for a quick call?"
First Attempt
"Hi! I was hoping to reach [Name] — is he/she available?"
If They Ask What It's About
"Sure! I'm reaching out from Infor — we work with distribution companies on warehouse technology. I had a quick question for [Name] about something I came across regarding [Company]. Do you know if he/she handles that side of things?"
If They Say He/She Is Busy
"No problem at all — is there a better time to reach him/her directly? Or would it be okay if I sent a quick email?"
Get the Email
"Perfect — what's the best email to reach [Name] at? I'll keep it short, I promise."
Short Voicemail (Under 20 seconds)
"Hey [Name], this is Brian from Infor — I'm reaching out because I work with distribution companies on warehouse technology and I had a quick question for you. I'll shoot you an email too, but feel free to call me back at [number]. Talk soon!"
Follow-Up Voicemail
"Hey [Name], Brian again from Infor — I know you're busy, I'll keep this short. I've left a couple messages and sent an email about how we've been helping distributors modernize their warehouse operations. If now's not the right time that's totally fine — just let me know and I'll follow up down the road. You can reach me at [number]. Thanks!"
Soft Meeting Ask
"I don't want to take up too much of your time today — but based on what you're sharing, I think it'd be worth a 30-minute call with one of our WMS specialists to go a little deeper. Would you be open to that?"
Give Two Options
"I have availability [Day] morning or [Day] afternoon — which works better for you?"
If They Hesitate
"Totally understand — no pressure at all. Even if the timing isn't right, would it be okay if I followed up with you in a few weeks? I want to make sure we stay on your radar."
Confirm & Close
"Perfect — I'll send a calendar invite right now with all the details. And if anything comes up, just let me know and we'll reschedule. Looking forward to it!"

Objection Handling
Common brush-offs and how to handle them
💬 "We're happy with our current WMS."
Your Rebuttal
"That's great to hear — I'm not here to disrupt what's working. I'm just curious, is your current system doing anything around AI-driven pick path optimization or predictive slotting? Those are areas where we've been seeing really meaningful results for distributors. Would you be open to a quick look just to benchmark where you stand?"
💬 "We just signed a contract with our current vendor."
Your Rebuttal
"Totally understand — timing is everything. When does that contract come up for renewal? I'd love to stay on your radar so when that conversation does happen, you have Infor in the mix. Would it be okay if I followed up with you closer to that window?"
💬 "We don't have budget for this right now."
Your Rebuttal
"Completely understand — and honestly that's where the ROI conversation becomes even more important. A lot of our customers found that Infor WMS paid for itself within the first year through labor savings and throughput gains alone. Would you be open to a 30-minute call just to understand what that could look like for your operation?"
💬 "That's not my decision — you'd need to talk to someone else."
Your Rebuttal
"I appreciate you being upfront — I'm not trying to go around anyone. Can I ask though, from your perspective on the operations side, have you felt any pain points with the current setup? I ask because having someone like you in the conversation who actually lives in the warehouse every day is really valuable. Would you be open to a quick call just to explore it together?"
💬 "Just send me some info and I'll take a look."
Your Rebuttal
"Absolutely — I'll send something over. To make sure I send you the most relevant information, can I ask what your biggest challenge in the warehouse is right now? That way I can tailor it to what matters most to you rather than sending a generic overview."
💬 "We're not looking at WMS right now — bad timing."
Your Rebuttal
"Totally fair — when do you think the timing would be better? I ask because a lot of companies we work with said the same thing, and then something happened — a big DC expansion, a labor issue, a missed shipment — that moved it up on the priority list. I'd rather be a resource for you before that happens than after."
💬 "We're probably too small for an enterprise WMS."
Your Rebuttal
"That's actually a really common misconception — Infor WMS is built to scale, which means it works really well for companies at your stage too. Some of our best-fit customers started with two or three DCs and grew into much larger operations on the same platform. Would it make sense to at least have a conversation and see if there's a fit?"
